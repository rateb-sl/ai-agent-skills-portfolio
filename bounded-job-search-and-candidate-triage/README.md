# Bounded Job Search and Candidate Triage

## What this skill does

This skill turns job-board browsing into a bounded research process. It helps a candidate search several sources, remove duplicates, reject infeasible roles, compare realistic matches, and receive a shortlist supported by visible evidence.

## Why it is valuable

Job boards contain stale listings, repeated postings, incomplete salary information, unclear work authorization, and roles that look relevant but fail important constraints. This workflow makes those risks explicit before the candidate invests time in an application.

It helps candidates:

- search with a finite budget instead of browsing endlessly;
- prioritize recent and relevant roles;
- separate strong matches from stretch opportunities;
- record exactly why a role was retained or rejected;
- preserve control over documents, applications, and outreach;
- use AI assistance without handing over credentials or private application data.

## Compatibility

The method is tool-agnostic and can be used manually, with Hermes, Claude, GPT-based agents, Gemini, browser assistants, or a simple spreadsheet. It works with public job boards and company career pages, subject to each site's access rules.

## Typical result

A completed run produces a short, deduplicated shortlist with direct URLs, fit bands, evidence labels, caveats, and one clear next verification step per role.
